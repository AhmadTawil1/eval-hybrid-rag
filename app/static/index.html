Key finding. Hybrid search found the most evidence, but with 40 scored questions the gain over dense is suggestive, not conclusive. The bigger finding is that search, not the language model, is the bottleneck: the top 5 results held only 17 to 26% of the evidence a question needs, so the model correctly refused more than half of the answerable questions. The technical report has the details.

How it works

  • Dense search finds passages by meaning, sparse by shared keywords (BM25), hybrid merges both with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • The top 5 passages go to gpt-5-mini, which must cite them or refuse. The scores above come from a separate, stronger judge model.
  • Answers take about 10 seconds because gpt-5-mini thinks before answering. Search itself takes well under 0.1 s.
Data: MultiHop-RAG (Tang and Yang, COLM 2024, ODC-BY). This demo has usage limits to keep its running cost fixed. Retrieval-only search is free.
